Headquarters Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, India

InAmigos Foundation is a registered Section 8 non-profit organisation, founded in September 2020 and licensed by the Central Government. Based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates across six areas of sustained social action — reaching communities in 28 states with a growing network of over 200 dedicated volunteers.

Holding 80G and 12A certifications, the foundation ensures full transparency and accountability for donors. Its CSR-1 registration enables partnerships with corporate entities, while NITI Aayog registration aligns its work with national development goals. An IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification underlines its commitment to operational excellence.
