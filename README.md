## 🌟 Task Overviews & Details

### 🔹 Task 1: InAmigos NGO Awareness Webpage
- **Objective:** Design and engineer an editorial-grade, human-centric single-page NGO awareness platform adhering to international design agency standards.
- **Key Highlights:**
  - Zero generic templates; built with semantic HTML5, CSS custom properties, and vanilla JS.
  - 10 distinct sections: Navigation, Hero, About Us, 6 Flagship Causes (Seva, Bachpan Shala, Jeev Daya, Udaan, Prakriti, Vikas), Impact Statistics, Featured Initiatives, Visual Story Gallery, Volunteer Action, Social Presence, Institutional Footer.
  - 100% verified non-profit credentials (Section 8, 12A, 80G, CSR-1, NITI Aayog Darpan).

### 🔹 Task 2: InAmigos Website Improvement Analysis
- **Objective:** Comprehensive UI/UX, conversion rate optimization (CRO), accessibility (WCAG 2.1), and security audit of `https://inamigosfoundation.org.in`.
- **Key Findings & Recommendations:**
  - Remediation of hidden spam/external injection links in the production DOM.
  - Fixing empty "Our Causes" grid and replacing walls of text with scannable cards.
  - Resolving WCAG color contrast failures (`#00CC83` text on white fail 2.2:1 ratio).
  - Streamlining donor journey from 4+ clicks to 1-click Razorpay payment gateway integration.

### 🔹 Task 3: AI Website Generation — Nexora AI Platform
- **Objective:** Build an autonomous enterprise AI multi-agent orchestration platform using modern generative design patterns.
- **Key Features:**
  - Dark-mode glassmorphic interface with animated live terminal console.
  - Interactive multi-scenario swarm playground (FinTech Risk, Supply Chain, Zero-Day Security, ESG Carbon Tracking).
  - Dynamic monthly/annual pricing calculator with real-time discounts and FAQ accordions.

### 🔹 Task 4: NGO Website Feature Highlight & Figma UI Spec
- **Objective:** Propose and design 5 mission-critical features that can be implemented directly on the InAmigos Foundation platform.
- **The 5 Engineered Features:**
  1. **Instant Transparent 80G Tax-Exempt Donation & Impact Visualizer:** Real-time outcome calculator (₹1,200 = 1 month education kit) with automated WhatsApp 80G receipts.
  2. **Interactive Volunteer Skill-Matching & Fast-Track Onboarding Portal:** 3-step widget matching student/professional skills to remote & ground cohorts.
  3. **Live Geospatial Impact Dashboard & Transparency Telemetry:** Real-time counters across 28 states with 1-click downloads for Form 10B/audited statements.
  4. **Verified Human Story Carousel:** Dignity-first beneficiary & volunteer stories with 1-click direct cause donation links.
  5. **Goal-Driven Urgent Campaign Hub:** Emergency relief crowdfunding cards with live progress bars and micro-updates.
