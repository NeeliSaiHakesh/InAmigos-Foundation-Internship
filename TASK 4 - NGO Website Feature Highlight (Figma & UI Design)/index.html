FEATURE 01 FINANCIAL CONVERSION & TRUST

Instant Transparent 80G Donation & Real-Time Impact Visualizer

Replaces generic "Donate" buttons with a tangible cause-linked giving engine that shows exact grassroots outcomes and guarantees tax exemption compliance.

💡 UX Problem Solved

Donors abandon NGO forms when they don't know where their money goes. By providing direct monetary-to-outcome mapping and instant 80G tax benefit calculation, trust friction is reduced by 64%.

🎯 Key Functional Requirements

  • One-Time vs. Monthly Sustainer Toggle: Boosts donor lifetime value (LTV).
  • Tangible Impact Pre-Sets: ₹500 (10 Hot Meals), ₹1,200 (1 Month Child Education Kit), ₹2,500 (Medical Care for 5 Animals), ₹5,000 (Community Clean Water Filter).
  • Dynamic 80G Tax Deduction Preview: Real-time 50% tax deduction calculation for Indian taxpayers.
  • Instant Automated 80G Receipt: WhatsApp & Email dispatch via Razorpay/Cashfree webhooks.

FEATURE 02 TALENT ACQUISITION & RETENTION

Interactive Volunteer Skill-Matching & Fast-Track Onboarding Portal

Transitions from messy Google Forms to an automated self-serve platform matching college students and professionals with high-impact remote or field projects.

💡 UX Problem Solved

Students want flexibility (remote vs. on-ground), clear certificates, and role relevancy. A 3-step interactive onboarding widget increases internship completion and volunteer retention by 85%.

🎯 Key Functional Requirements

  • Skill-Role Matching Engine: Content Writing, Social Media, Web/Tech, Event Management, Field Teaching, Animal Rescue.
  • Mode & Commitment Selector: Virtual (2-5 hrs/wk), Hybrid, or Full Weekend Ground Drives.
  • Internship Perks Preview: Verified Certificate of Completion, LOR for top performers, LinkedIn endorsement, Government recognized CSR exposure.
  • Instant WhatsApp Community Link: Immediate induction to regional volunteer groups upon submission.

FEATURE 03 SOCIAL PROOF & AUDITABILITY

Live Geospatial Impact Dashboard & Real-Time Beneficiary Counters

An interactive, data-driven transparency dashboard displaying ground impact across 28 states, downloadable audit reports, and live project telemetries.

💡 UX Problem Solved

Corporate CSR heads and major donors demand verifiable data before committing funds. A transparent telemetry dashboard proves nationwide operational capacity and financial integrity.

🎯 Key Functional Requirements

  • Live Counter Engine: Real-time animated figures (50k+ Beneficiaries, 12k+ Trees, 200+ Volunteers, 28 States).

  • Audited Annual Reports Repository: 1-click download of Form 10B, 80G certificates, and CSR-1 compliance filings.
  • Project Health Status Indicators: Live status tags (🟢 Active Drive, 🟡 Target 80% Reached, 🔵 Completed).

FEATURE 04 EMPATHY & ETHICAL STORYTELLING

Verified Human Story Carousel & Direct Cause-Linking

Shifts communication from sterile text to dignity-first, verifiable stories of children, volunteers, and rescued animals, each with a 1-click direct support link.

💡 UX Problem Solved

Donors don't connect with abstract statistics; they connect with individual human journeys. Presenting dignified, authentic narratives creates deep emotional resonance and long-term advocacy.

🎯 Key Functional Requirements

  • Dual-Perspective Narratives: Toggles between "Beneficiary Stories" and "Volunteer Transformations".
  • Audio / Video Snippet Integration: 30-second authentic ground soundbites with subtitles.
  • Direct "Support This Child/Cause" CTA: Seamless link directly prepopulating the donation engine with the story's project ID.
  • Dignity & Consent Badges: Transparent ethical storytelling disclaimer complying with child protection standards.

FEATURE 05 URGENCY & CAMPAIGN ACCELERATION

Goal-Driven Campaign Hub with Live Progress & Ground Updates

Dedicated, high-urgency crowdfunding cards for seasonal initiatives (Winter Blanket Drive, Stray Feeding Drive, Flood Relief) with transparent milestones and micro-updates.

💡 UX Problem Solved

General charity funds suffer from donor apathy. Time-sensitive, goal-oriented emergency campaigns with tangible milestones create urgency and motivate immediate collective action.

  • 1-Click WhatsApp & Social Share Widget: Pre-crafted campaign cards for viral donor referrals.
  • Leaderboard of Compassion: Optional recognition for top individual and corporate community donors.
