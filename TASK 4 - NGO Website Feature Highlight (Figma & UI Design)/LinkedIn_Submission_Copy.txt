🚀 Excited to share my completion of TASK 4 as part of my internship at InAmigos Foundation! 🎨💻

📌 Task: NGO Website Feature Highlight & UX Architecture (Figma Task)
🏢 Organization: InAmigos Foundation ("Uniting Minds for Change")

For this task, I conducted an in-depth UX architecture analysis and created a comprehensive high-fidelity Figma UI design specification highlighting 5 MISSION-CRITICAL FEATURES that can dramatically elevate an NGO's digital impact and conversion:

1️⃣ Instant Transparent 80G Tax-Exempt Donation & Real-Time Outcome Engine:
• Direct monetary-to-outcome mapping (e.g. ₹1,200 = 1 month education kit for a child).
• Real-time 50% Section 80G tax deduction calculator.
• Automated 80G tax receipt dispatch via WhatsApp & email upon checkout.

2️⃣ Interactive Volunteer Skill-Matching & Fast-Track Portal:
• Multi-step skill-to-role matching (Content, Design, Social Media, Field Rescue).
• Instant onboarding with automated WhatsApp community induction.

3️⃣ Live Geospatial Impact Dashboard & Transparency Telemetry:
• Real-time counters across 28 states & 50,000+ beneficiaries.
• Downloadable audited financial statements (Form 10B, 12A, CSR-1) for maximum CSR credibility.

4️⃣ Verified Human Story Carousel & Direct Support Linking:
• Dignity-first beneficiary & volunteer transformation narratives with 1-click cause donation links.

5️⃣ Goal-Driven Campaign Hub with Live Progress & Ground Micro-Updates:
• High-urgency crowdfunding cards with live progress bars, donor count, and field photo logs.

✨ Deliverables Generated:
• High-Fidelity Figma UI Architecture & Interactive Prototype
• Complete Design Specification PDF & DOCX Process Reports
• Multi-device responsive exports (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile)

A huge thank you to Govind Shukla and the team at InAmigos Foundation for this insightful product design assignment!
